# GCFIOS Market Thesis Architecture

## Market Thesis
The Market Thesis is the central organizing structure of GCFIOS.

## Thesis Layers
- Macro Thesis
- Alignment Thesis
- Structural Thesis
- Continuation Thesis
- Participation Thesis
- Execution Thesis
- Portfolio Thesis
- Learning Thesis

## Role of the Thesis
Each engine updates, validates, refines, or challenges one part of the thesis.
