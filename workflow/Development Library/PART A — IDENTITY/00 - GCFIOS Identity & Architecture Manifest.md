# GCFIOS Identity & Architecture Manifest

## Core Philosophy
GCFIOS is built around a central operating concept: the Market Thesis.

## Core Domain
The central domain object of GCFIOS is the Market Thesis.

Every engine exists to:
- Create
- Validate
- Refine
- Challenge
- Execute
- Manage
- Learn from

a Market Thesis.

Reports, dashboards, trade tickets, and alerts are views over that thesis.
The thesis is the reality.
