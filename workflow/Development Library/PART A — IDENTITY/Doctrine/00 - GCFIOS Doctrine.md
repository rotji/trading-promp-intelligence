# GCFIOS Doctrine

## Purpose
This document is the constitutional layer of GCFIOS.

## Core Principles
1. The market is never assumed; it is investigated.
2. Every conclusion must be evidence based.
3. The system builds and maintains Market Theses.
4. Execution never creates a thesis; it implements an approved thesis.
5. Capital follows opportunity, and opportunity follows evidence.
6. Every module has exactly one responsibility.
7. Every engine either creates, updates, validates, challenges, executes, monitors, or learns from a Market Thesis.

## Constitutional Role
This doctrine governs all future implementation layers, including MT5 components, Node services, React interfaces, AI agents, and any future platform technology.
