# GCFIOS Operating Model

## Purpose
This document defines the permanent operational domains of GCFIOS before any daily workflow is written.

## Operating Model Principle
The operating model defines what exists and how domains interact.
The operational workflow defines what happens first, second, and third.

## Permanent Operational Domains
1. Investigation Domain
   - Responsible for gathering evidence and market context.
2. Thesis Domain
   - Responsible for building and updating the Market Thesis.
3. Opportunity Domain
   - Responsible for converting a thesis into tradable opportunities.
4. Execution Domain
   - Responsible for turning approved opportunities into broker-ready actions.
5. Portfolio Domain
   - Responsible for managing exposure and portfolio consequences.
6. Learning Domain
   - Responsible for capturing outcomes and improving future decisions.

## Domain Interaction
Investigation
↓
Thesis
↓
Opportunity
↓
Execution
↓
Portfolio
↓
Learning

## Technology Mapping
Each domain may later be implemented by:
- MT5 libraries, scripts, services, or expert advisors
- Node services or APIs
- React dashboards or views
- AI agents or assistants

## Architectural Outcome
This operating model becomes the structural foundation from which the daily operational workflow is derived.
