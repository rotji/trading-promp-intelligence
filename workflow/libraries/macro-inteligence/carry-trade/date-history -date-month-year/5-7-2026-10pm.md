OVERALL ASSESSMENT
========================================================

The global carry trade remains active.

Although the Bank of Japan has continued its monetary normalization and Japanese authorities remain highly sensitive to further Yen weakness, the interest-rate differential between Japan and the United States remains sufficiently wide to keep carry trades economically attractive.

However, intervention risk and carry unwind risk have increased compared with previous months.
