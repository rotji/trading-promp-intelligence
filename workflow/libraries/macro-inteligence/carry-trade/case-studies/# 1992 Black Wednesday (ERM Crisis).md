========================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
========================================================

Black Wednesday, which occurred on 16 September 1992, remains one of the most significant currency crises in modern financial history.

The crisis developed because the United Kingdom attempted to maintain the British Pound within the European Exchange Rate Mechanism (ERM), a system designed to keep European currencies trading within predetermined exchange-rate bands before the creation of the Euro.

To remain inside the ERM, the Bank of England was required to defend the Pound whenever market forces pushed it below its permitted exchange-rate range.

However, the United Kingdom's domestic economic conditions increasingly conflicted with the monetary policy required by the ERM.

The British economy was weakening.

Inflation remained elevated.

Interest rates were high.

Economic growth slowed.

Markets gradually concluded that the Pound was fundamentally overvalued.

Large hedge funds, investment banks, and institutional investors began aggressively selling Pounds while purchasing stronger currencies, particularly the German Deutsche Mark.

Among the most famous participants was George Soros' Quantum Fund, which established one of the largest speculative short positions in financial history.

The Bank of England attempted to defend the Pound by purchasing billions of Pounds in the foreign exchange market while simultaneously raising interest rates.

Despite these efforts, speculative selling overwhelmed official intervention.

Eventually, the UK government withdrew from the ERM.

The Pound immediately depreciated significantly.

Although initially viewed as a national financial embarrassment, the depreciation ultimately improved the competitiveness of the British economy and contributed to a stronger economic recovery in subsequent years.

Black Wednesday demonstrated that central banks cannot indefinitely maintain exchange-rate policies that conflict with underlying economic fundamentals.

========================================================
LESSONS LEARNED
========================================================

Black Wednesday demonstrated that:

• Markets can overwhelm central-bank intervention.

• Exchange rates cannot remain permanently detached from economic fundamentals.

• Capital flows ultimately determine currency equilibrium.

• Policy credibility is essential for exchange-rate stability.

• Large speculative positioning can accelerate market adjustments.

• Foreign exchange markets rapidly price macroeconomic imbalances.

========================================================
