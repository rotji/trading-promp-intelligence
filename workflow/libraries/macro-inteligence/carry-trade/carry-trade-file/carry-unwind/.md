# 6. Carry Unwind

## Introduction

A carry unwind occurs when investors rapidly reduce or completely close existing carry trade positions.

Unlike carry contraction, which develops gradually as market conditions become less favourable, a carry unwind is characterised by active position liquidation and accelerated capital repatriation.

Carry unwinds often produce sharp movements across multiple asset classes because the same capital that previously flowed into higher-return investments is simultaneously withdrawn.

Within GCFIOS, a carry unwind is classified as one of the most significant global liquidity contraction events because it can rapidly transmit financial stress across currencies, bonds, equities, commodities, credit markets, and cryptocurrencies.

---

## Fundamental Principle

A carry unwind occurs when:

The perceived risk of maintaining carry positions becomes greater than the expected return from those positions.

As this balance shifts, investors seek to preserve capital by reducing leverage, repaying borrowed funds, and exiting higher-risk investments.

---

## What Happens During A Carry Unwind

A typical carry unwind follows this sequence:

Carry Position

↓

Risk Increases

↓

Investors Reduce Exposure

↓

Assets Are Sold

↓

Funding Currency Is Purchased To Repay Loans

↓

Funding Currency Appreciates

↓

Investment Assets Decline

↓

Liquidity Contracts

↓

Volatility Increases

---

## Common Causes

Carry unwinds may be triggered by one or more of the following:

• Unexpected central bank policy changes

• Sudden increases in funding costs

• Sharp rises in market volatility

• Geopolitical shocks

• Financial crises

• Banking system stress

• Credit market deterioration

• Liquidity shortages

• Severe risk-off sentiment

• Forced deleveraging

Often, several of these factors occur simultaneously.

---

## Warning Signs

Carry unwinds rarely occur without warning.

Common warning signals include:

• Rising funding costs

• Increasing market volatility

• Tightening liquidity

• Widening credit spreads

• Safe-haven buying

• Rapid appreciation of funding currencies

• Declining risk appetite

• Falling equity market breadth

• Increasing demand for cash

The presence of multiple warning signs increases the probability of a carry unwind.

---

## Market Behaviour During A Carry Unwind

Carry unwinds often produce synchronized moves across markets.

Typical characteristics include:

• Funding currencies strengthen.

• High-yield currencies weaken.

• Equity markets decline.

• Growth assets underperform.

• Credit spreads widen.

• Market volatility rises.

• Liquidity deteriorates.

• Safe-haven demand increases.

Price movements frequently become larger and faster than during normal market conditions.

## Typical Beneficiaries

Carry unwinds often benefit:

• Funding currencies

• Safe-haven currencies

• High-quality government bonds

• Cash

• Defensive assets

The exact beneficiaries depend on the underlying cause of the unwind.

---

## Typical Vulnerable Assets

Carry unwinds often pressure:

• High-yield currencies

• Emerging market currencies

• Growth equities

• High-beta stocks

• Commodities

• Corporate credit

• Cryptocurrencies

• Leveraged investment strategies

Assets that previously benefited most from carry expansion often experience the largest declines during an unwind.

---

## Historical Characteristics

Historically, major carry unwinds have shared several common features:

• Rapid repricing

• Elevated volatility

• Large cross-market correlations

• Forced deleveraging

• Liquidity shortages

• Flight to quality

Although every unwind is unique, these characteristics frequently appear across different market cycles.

---

## Difference Between Contraction And Unwind

Carry Contraction

• Gradual process

• Slowing new carry activity

• Reduced expansion

• Lower confidence

• Orderly market adjustment

Carry Unwind

• Active liquidation

• Existing positions closed

• Rapid capital reversal

• Elevated volatility

• Disorderly market conditions

Contraction may continue for weeks or months.

A carry unwind can develop within hours or days.
